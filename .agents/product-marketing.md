# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-04*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** NIOS 10th & Plus Two with warm human companionship for learners who felt stuck—not a coaching factory.

**Emotional core:** Audience can self-learn but feels they failed in life/career; warmth and non-judgment drive choice over cheap alternatives. ~₹20K premium = investment in not walking alone until pass.

**What it does:** Skillvard helps Kerala students complete NIOS open schooling with admission guidance, subject selection, exam support, recorded classes, short notes, WhatsApp doubt clearance, and career-readiness extras (English, personality, mock interviews).

**Product category:** Open schooling / NIOS admission & learning support (Kerala)

**Product type:** Education service (learning support center)

**Business model:** Course fees; NIOS Plus Two as launch offer; future skill-based courses

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** N/A (B2C)

**Decision-makers:** Students (16–25) and parents

**Primary use case:** Complete Plus Two after failure, dropout, health/family constraints, or work schedule conflicts

**Jobs to be done:**
- Pass NIOS Plus Two without repeating rigid school/coaching trauma
- Choose the right stream for career goals
- Get affordable, flexible study support until exams

**Use cases:**
- Failed Plus Two seeking a second chance
- Cannot attend regular school (health, family, distance)
- Working students needing self-paced learning
- Parents worried about exam fear and cost

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Traditional schooling/coaching feels heavy, expensive, and shame-filled after failure.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Coaching centers: high pressure, high fees, not built for dropouts
- Self-study alone: no structure, no doubt support, exam fear remains

**What it costs them:** Lost confidence, delayed careers, years stuck without Plus Two certificate

**Emotional tension:** Fear of failing again, shame, anxiety about future

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Local Plus Two coaching centers — rigid schedules, exam fear culture

**Secondary:** Distance/online-only content — little hand-holding or WhatsApp support

**Indirect:** Giving up on formal education — limits jobs and higher studies

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Not a coaching center — supportive NIOS path
- Support until you pass exams
- Flexible, self-paced + WhatsApp doubt clearance
- AI notes, recorded classes, exam-focused live sessions
- Affordable; career extras beyond certificate

**How we do it differently:** Walk with students per subject until exams; Malayalam + English notes; smart app recordings

**Why customers choose us:** Second chance without judgment; practical support; clear streams and documents guidance

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| NIOS is not as good as regular board | NIOS is National Institute of Open Schooling — recognized path; we guide admission and exams properly |
| I failed before — will I fail again? | Flexible pacing, fear-free methods, support until pass |
| Too expensive | Positioned as affordable vs coaching + tuition + books bundle |

**Anti-persona:** Students wanting guaranteed marks without study; those needing full-time residential school only

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Coaching pressure, failures, can't attend school, cost

**Pull:** Flexible learning, WhatsApp support, career extras, "rewrite success" messaging

**Habit:** Trusting only traditional coaching; fear of open schooling

**Anxiety:** Whether NIOS is valid; whether online/support is enough
